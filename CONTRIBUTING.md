# Contributing to Open Auto Trade

Open Auto Trade is a free, open-source automotive discovery, import-planning and ownership information project for Africa. **Contributors from every country are welcome.** No citizenship or passport requirement.

## Ways to contribute

- Suggest a reputable **non-auction** dealer or exporter website with evidence of its public listings.
- Add or correct official customs, vehicle import and registration resources for African destinations.
- Suggest a vehicle freight carrier, freight forwarder, inspection provider, licensed payment resource, spare-parts catalogue or workshop.
- Report broken links, unsupported country claims or outdated guidance.
- Improve mobile accessibility, localization, low-bandwidth performance, documentation and code.

[Submit a provider](https://github.com/ekamaw-ux/open-auto-trade/issues/new/choose) or open a pull request with the proposed change.

## Evidence required

Include the provider's official URL, service category, known origin/destination coverage, evidence URL and date checked. **Unknown coverage is acceptable; unsupported claims are not.** For customs and import requirements, prefer the destination's official government source. Follow [directory verification standards](DIRECTORY_VERIFICATION.md).

The project does not endorse companies merely because they appear in a directory. Do not represent listed organizations as partners without authorization.

## Safety and data rights

- Never post passports, identity documents, customer information, bank credentials, passwords, API keys or private supplier contacts.
- Never scrape, mirror or redistribute third-party listings, images or pricing without permission.
- Do not add auction listings to the dealer discovery directory.
- Distinguish estimates from official duties, live inventory, booking availability and binding quotations.
- Never copy MaxBid private data, models, assessments or proprietary information.
- For code changes, explain what changed and how it was tested.

## Community standards

Be respectful. Harassment and discrimination are not welcome. Maintainers may decline unverifiable, unsafe, promotional or off-scope submissions.
