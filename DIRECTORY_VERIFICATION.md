# Directory verification and route coverage

Open Auto Trade is a free directory, not a freight broker, payment processor, inspection company or escrow service.

## Status vocabulary
- **Official authority**: government or intergovernmental site; verify current regulations directly.
- **Provider website**: public service-provider homepage, not evidence of coverage of a particular corridor.
- **Coverage unverified**: do not claim service in a destination or quote a shipping price without written confirmation.
- **Not a partner**: listing a provider does not create an endorsement or commercial relationship.

## What we verified
- UN sources list 54 African UN member countries. The destination picker includes all 54.
- Wise publishes a current supported destination list: https://wise.com/help/articles/2571942/what-countriesregions-can-i-send-to . Its availability depends on destination, currency and payment type; do not label Wise as universally available.
- GitHub supports structured issue templates: https://docs.github.com/en/communities/using-templates-to-encourage-useful-issues-and-pull-requests/configuring-issue-templates-for-your-repository .

## Before promoting a provider to route-verified
1. Verify the exact official domain and provider identity.
2. Confirm origin, destination, cargo type and import/export eligibility from a current provider document or written quote.
3. For payments, confirm sending and receiving country, business versus personal account restrictions, currency and beneficiary requirements.
4. For inspections, confirm on-site coverage and deliverable report scope.
5. Record source URL and verification date; recheck after material changes.
6. Never copy third-party vehicle listings, prices or images without permission; no auction-only sources.

## Outstanding checks
Most individual shipping corridors, inspection site coverage and payment business-use restrictions remain **unverified**. The website must not describe these as confirmed partner routes.

## Source-backed coverage check — 2026-11-05

- **Wise supported recipient countries:** Official Wise list names Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Morocco, Nigeria, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda and Zambia. This is not approval for vehicle purchase, business transfer, or any specific sending country. Source: https://wise.com/help/articles/2571942/what-countriesregions-can-i-send-to
- **Wise excluded locations:** Official Wise availability page excludes Burundi, Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, DR Congo, Eritrea, Libya, Somalia, South Sudan and Sudan. These must not be presented as Wise payment destinations. Source: https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/where-can-i-use-wise
- **Höegh carrier network:** Official port directory includes Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, Tema, Lagos, Durban and other African ports. Port directory presence alone does not establish a bookable voyage or direct route. Source: https://www.hoeghautoliners.com/offices-agents-and-ports
- **Höegh trade lanes:** Official site describes East Asia to Africa and Middle East to Africa trade lanes, and a Middle East/India to Africa route with Durban as a discharge port. Source: https://www.hoeghautoliners.com/trade-routes and https://www.hoeghautoliners.com/trade-routes/middle-east-to-africa-and-americas
- **Inspection:** No site-level inspection availability has been confirmed for individual vehicles. Require a written quote and report scope before labeling a route verified.

**Important:** This check verifies selected public evidence, not every provider across all 54 countries. Country-specific import laws and commercial eligibility require further primary-source review.

## Additional source checks — 2026-11-05

- **Freight quote workflow:** Höegh Autoliners describes checking a sailing schedule, submitting a freight inquiry, receiving a quote, then booking and tracking. This does **not** establish any freight amount or a guaranteed sailing. https://www.hoeghautoliners.com/
- **Korea used-vehicle inspection:** SGS Korea describes independent used-vehicle inspection, damage photographs and reports. Confirm whether a one-off pre-purchase inspection at the seller's site is offered. https://www.sgs.com/en-kr/services/used-vehicle-inspection
- **Japan used-vehicle inspection:** SGS Japan describes used-vehicle inspection services, but not guaranteed coverage for any individual dealer's stock. https://www.sgs.com/ja-jp/services/used-vehicle-inspection-migrated
- **Egypt import advisory:** U.S. International Trade Administration reports general restrictions on used-car imports with exceptions; confirm current official rules and vehicle eligibility before purchasing. https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/egypt-prohibited-restricted-imports
- **Rwanda import advisory:** ITA's March 2026 guide details vehicle emissions documentation requirements. https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/rwanda-import-requirements-and-documentation
- **Angola import advisory:** ITA describes vehicle age restrictions by vehicle class. Confirm the current rules with Angolan customs. https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/angola-prohibited-restricted-imports
- **Ghana customs valuation:** ITA cautions that used-car customs valuation is complex and can change. https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/ghana-customs-regulations

### Still unverified
No real-time freight rates, confirmed bookable origin-destination voyages, inspection appointments, dealer-specific inspection prices or complete vehicle import rules for all 54 destinations are available. The directory must not claim these as verified.
