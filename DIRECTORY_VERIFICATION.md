# Directory verification and route coverage

Open Auto Trade is a free directory, not a freight broker, payment processor, inspection company or escrow service.

## Status vocabulary
- **Official authority**: government or intergovernmental site; verify current regulations directly.
- **Provider website**: public service-provider homepage, not evidence of coverage of a particular corridor.
- **Coverage unverified**: do not claim service in a destination or quote a shipping price without written confirmation.
- **Not a partner**: listing a provider does not create an endorsement or commercial relationship.

## What we verified
- UN sources list 54 African UN member countries. The destination picker includes all 54.
- Wise publishes a current supported destination list: https://wise.com/help/articles/2571942/what-countriesregions-can-i-send-to . Its availability depends on destination, currency and payment type; do not label Wise as universally available.
- GitHub supports structured issue templates: https://docs.github.com/en/communities/using-templates-to-encourage-useful-issues-and-pull-requests/configuring-issue-templates-for-your-repository .

## Before promoting a provider to route-verified
1. Verify the exact official domain and provider identity.
2. Confirm origin, destination, cargo type and import/export eligibility from a current provider document or written quote.
3. For payments, confirm sending and receiving country, business versus personal account restrictions, currency and beneficiary requirements.
4. For inspections, confirm on-site coverage and deliverable report scope.
5. Record source URL and verification date; recheck after material changes.
6. Never copy third-party vehicle listings, prices or images without permission; no auction-only sources.

## Outstanding checks
Most individual shipping corridors, inspection site coverage and payment business-use restrictions remain **unverified**. The website must not describe these as confirmed partner routes.
