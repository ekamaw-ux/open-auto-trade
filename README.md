# Open Auto Trade

**Making global car buying and ownership more accessible across Africa.**

Open Auto Trade is a **free, open-source automotive discovery and import-planning platform** helping people across Africa find international vehicle dealers, explore shipping options, understand import requirements, and access ownership resources.

From Japan and South Korea to the UAE, Europe and North America, our goal is to make automotive information easier to find, understand and use.

**Free to explore. Open to contribute. Built for African mobility.**

[Explore the website](https://open-auto-trade.vercel.app/) · [Suggest a provider or correction](https://github.com/ekamaw-ux/open-auto-trade/issues/new/choose) · [Contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

## What you can do today

- Browse links to independent, publicly accessible international dealer and exporter websites. **No auction listings are included in the directory.**
- Explore vehicle freight companies and contact them directly about RoRo or container shipping.
- Select from **54 African destinations** to start an import journey and locate customs guidance.
- Use a manual landed-cost planning calculator and compare two vehicles. **Estimates are user-entered, not official duty assessments or live quotations.**
- Check VIN information and manufacturer/model data using publicly available NHTSA services, where supported.
- Find links to independent inspection, payment and customs resources.
- Suggest providers, report broken links and contribute improvements through GitHub.

## Our mission

We believe access to automotive information shouldn't depend on expensive subscriptions, exclusive industry connections or where someone lives.

Open Auto Trade is built openly for African buyers, with contributions welcome from around the world. The long-term vision includes better spare-parts discovery, repair resources and practical ownership guidance.

## Important boundaries

**Open Auto Trade is an independent information and planning directory, not a vehicle seller, broker, freight carrier, payment processor, escrow provider or customs authority.** Inclusion in the directory does not mean endorsement, verification of an individual transaction or a commercial partnership.

Vehicle availability, dealer status, shipping routes, inspection coverage, payment eligibility, import restrictions, taxes and fees must be confirmed with the relevant provider or authority before any purchase or payment. Linked sites may have their own fees and terms.

There are **no authorized live dealer inventory feeds, automated country-specific duty calculations, shipment bookings, payment processing or verified repair-garage network** in this release. We link to external services rather than copying their inventory.

## Project and governance

Founded by **Elvis Kamau**. Initially stewarded by **Sahara Trade Hub LLC**. Open Auto Trade is a distinct public project, separate from MaxBid and its private assessments, data and operations.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build tools or secrets are required for the static website. Optional API endpoints under `api/` are deployed through Vercel.
