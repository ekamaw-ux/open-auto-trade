04 / Verify before you pay

Import smarter. Avoid surprises.

STEP 01

Check eligibility

Confirm vehicle age limits, steering-side requirements, emissions rules and inspection requirements with the destination country's official agencies.

STEP 02

Verify the vehicle

Request the VIN/chassis number, export certificate, inspection report, clear title and independent condition evidence before sending funds.

STEP 03

Verify the seller

Independently confirm business registration, bank beneficiary, shipping documents and written refund terms. Avoid payments to unrelated personal accounts.
